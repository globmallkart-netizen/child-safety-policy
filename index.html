Child Safety Standards

Effective Date: June 2026

Gurav Vivah ("the App") is committed to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) of any kind.

Our Commitment

We strictly prohibit child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any content that exploits, sexualizes, or endangers minors. The App is intended solely for use by adults seeking matrimonial profile creation and is not designed for or targeted at children.

Prevention Measures

  • Users must confirm they are 18 years of age or older to use the App.
  • We do not permit content depicting or sexualizing minors.
  • We monitor for and remove any content that violates this policy.

Reporting Child Safety Concerns

If you encounter any content or behavior that may violate child safety standards, please report it immediately:

  • Email: globalmkart@gmail.com
  • In-app: Use the "Report" option available within the App

Compliance with Authorities

We comply with all applicable child protection laws and cooperate with law enforcement and relevant national/regional authorities, including reporting to NCMEC (National Center for Missing & Exploited Children) or equivalent bodies where legally required.
